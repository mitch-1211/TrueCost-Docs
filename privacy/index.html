Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

TrueCost is designed to help users compare the cost of different vehicle ownership scenarios. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the minimum data required to improve the app.

Data We Collect

  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous information about how the app is used (e.g. screens viewed, actions taken).
  • Crash Data: If the app crashes, we collect diagnostic information to help fix issues.

How We Collect Data

We use third-party services including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect this information.

How We Use Data

  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To identify and fix crashes or bugs

Data Linking

We do not collect personally identifiable information and do not link data to individual users.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via the App Store listing.
